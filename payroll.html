The week

Hours come from the Time Clock — every pair of in and out. A punch with no matching out is never guessed at; it's flagged and left out of the total until somebody fixes it on the clock. A rate nobody has set is left blank rather than filled with a number we made up.

How this week is worked out

Off by default. Federal overtime applies to non-exempt staff — confirm with whoever runs your payroll before switching it on.
Approving a week writes it into Books & Margins as a Payroll expense so the month's real labour cost is in the books. It stops at gross pay: Accelerated Experiences is not your payroll provider and doesn't calculate withholding.
